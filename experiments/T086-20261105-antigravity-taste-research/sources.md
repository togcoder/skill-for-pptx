## Domain 2: Vector Graphics, Iconography & SVG Craft

8. **Google Material Symbols & Icon Design Guidelines**
   - **URL:** https://m3.material.io/styles/icons/designing-icons
   - **Accessed:** 2026-11-05
   - **License:** Apache-2.0.
   - **Peer Authority:** World's most widely deployed icon system (billions of Android, Chrome, and web surfaces).
   - **Core Principles Extracted:** Strict $24\times 24\text{dp}$ grid with $2\text{dp}$ padding (live area $20\times 20\text{dp}$); keyline shapes (circle $\varnothing 20\text{dp}$, square $18\times 18\text{dp}$, vertical rectangle $16\times 20\text{dp}$, horizontal rectangle $20\times 16\text{dp}$) to ensure identical optical weight; consistent $2\text{dp}$ stroke width; round stroke caps and joins.

9. **Apple SF Symbols 6 Design Specifications**
   - **URL:** https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/sf-symbols
   - **Accessed:** 2026-11-05
   - **License:** Proprietary Apple Inc. Developer Guidelines.
   - **Peer Authority:** System iconography standard across iOS, iPadOS, macOS, watchOS, and visionOS.
   - **Core Principles Extracted:** Optical alignment with typography cap-height and baseline; 9 optical weights (Ultralight to Black) matching San Francisco font weights; 3 scales (Small, Medium, Large); hierarchical multi-layer color rendering ($100\%$ primary, $30\%$ secondary, $15\%$ tertiary).

10. **Lucide Icons Design Principles**
    - **URL:** https://lucide.dev/guide/design/icon-design-guide
    - **Accessed:** 2026-11-05
    - **License:** ISC License.
    - **Peer Authority:** 14,000+ GitHub stars; community standard for modern React, Vue, and Svelte ecosystems.
    - **Core Principles Extracted:** $24\times 24\text{px}$ viewport; non-scaling $2\text{px}$ uniform stroke; sub-pixel grid snapping on integer coordinates ($0.5\text{px}$ offset for centered strokes); elimination of unnecessary anchor points to guarantee smooth bezier curvature.

11. **The Noun Project Icon Standardization Manual**
    - **URL:** https://thenounproject.com/handbook/
    - **Accessed:** 2026-11-05
    - **License:** Public educational resource.
    - **Peer Authority:** Repository of over 5 million vector glyphs used by designers worldwide.
    - **Core Principles Extracted:** Silhouette test (if an icon's solid black silhouette cannot be recognized at $16\text{px}$, the design has failed); optical balance over mathematical center (adjusting visual center of mass for triangles and asymmetric shapes); negative space ratio maintained at $35\%-45\%$.

12. **W3C Scalable Vector Graphics (SVG) 2 Specification (Geometry & Curves)**
    - **URL:** https://www.w3.org/TR/SVG2/paths.html
    - **Accessed:** 2026-11-05
    - **License:** W3C Software and Document Notice.
    - **Peer Authority:** Official web standard for vector mathematics.
    - **Core Principles Extracted:** Smooth cubic bezier continuity ($S$ command enforcing collinear control points for $C^1$ continuity); avoiding degenerate zero-length sub-paths; arc parametrization conversions.

## Domain 8: Data Visualization & Infographics

44. **Edward R. Tufte — *The Visual Display of Quantitative Information* (1983)**
    - **URL:** https://www.edwardtufte.com/tufte/books_vdqi
    - **Accessed:** 2026-11-05
    - **License:** Graphics Press LLC. Seminal foundational text.
    - **Peer Authority:** Universally recognized as the most important book ever written on data graphics.
    - **Core Principles Extracted:**
      - Data-Ink Ratio formula: $\text{Data-Ink Ratio} = \frac{\text{Data-Ink}}{\text{Total Ink Used to Print the Graphic}} = 1 - \text{Erasable Ink Fraction}$.
      - Eliminate "chartjunk": moiré vibrations, dark heavy gridlines, fake 3D bars/shadows, and decorative illustrations masquerading as data.
      - Above all else, show the data. Maximize the data-ink ratio. Revise and edit.

45. **Claus O. Wilke — *Fundamentals of Data Visualization* (2019)**
    - **URL:** https://clauswilke.com/dataviz/
    - **Accessed:** 2026-11-05
    - **License:** CC-BY-NC-ND 4.0 / O'Reilly Media.
    - **Peer Authority:** Standard university textbook on modern data visualization.
    - **Core Principles Extracted:**
      - Principle of Proportional Ink: The sizes of shaded areas in a visualization must be strictly proportional to the data values they represent.
      - Bar charts MUST start at 0; truncated baselines violate proportional ink.
      - For logarithmic axes, never use shaded bars; use dots/points where position encodes value.
      - In bubble/circle charts, area (not radius) must scale proportionally to value: $r \propto \sqrt{V}$.
