# Lead note on these files (T088)

These 13 files were written by Antigravity as `knowledge/taste/` in T086. The lead
merged them here, as **research notes**, not under `knowledge/`, for three reasons:

1. **They are written as formulas.** The ideas are often good, but they come wrapped in LaTeX,
   EMU arithmetic and OOXML tags. D011 asks for taste guidance in plain words that a weak model
   can follow.
2. **Several domains drift away from slides.** Examples: the BRDF lighting model (3D), G0–G3
   curvature continuity (CAD), Green's theorem centroids, Voronoi relaxation.
3. **No rule has passed a blind viewer test.** D010 requires one before a rule enters the skill.

They are raw material for T087, the PPTX skill written as guidance. When a rule is used there, it
is restated in plain words and cites the source in `../sources.md`.

Worth reading first, because these ideas recur across domains:

- one idea and one moving subject per slide;
- the three-second glance test;
- proximity over containers;
- one elevation per element (a border or a soft shadow, not both);
- bars start at zero, and labels sit next to their data;
- Morph only between shapes of the same type, with text kept out of a morphing card;
- the eye needs time to settle after a big move.
