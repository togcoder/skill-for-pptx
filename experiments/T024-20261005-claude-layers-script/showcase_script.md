This is how we improve every quarter.
[click] Our improvement cycle has four stages around customer value: plan, do, check and act.

[click] Do means we run small pilots instead of big launches.
[click] Check compares the pilots with the targets — last year 85% of pilots hit them.
[click] Act turns the winners into the new standard.
[click] And then the cycle starts again.
