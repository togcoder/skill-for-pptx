# T069 — beat sheet for the directed deck

**Deck:** `artifacts/T069-directed-morph.pptx` (14 slides, 9 click groups,
4 Morph transitions). **Base:** T067 directed copy
`experiments/T067-.../motion/artifacts/T066-deck-directed.pptx`.
**Date:** 2026-11-04.

The rule is simple: a slide opens on a state the presenter can talk over, then
**one** thing changes. A change is either a click or a Morph, never a click plus
a Morph plus a decoration. Four state pairs carry the argument:

| Pair | From → to | What continues (same `!!` object/idea) | Why it is a Morph, not an effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | slide 3 → 4 | the two answer cards and the question mark | the two answers become the winning answer; the cards move and swap weight |
| B | slide 5 → 6 | the dialectic circle, the straight line, the two cards | the two worldviews stay on screen; one grows, one recedes |
| C | slide 8 → 9 | the three part cards | the three parts become one whole; the cards move into a triangle |
| D | slide 12 → 13 | the quotation lines and the quotation mark | the quotation shrinks to a citation; the thesis takes the frame |

No background Morph, no thread Morph, no Morph between unrelated slides.

## Image laws applied

- **Identity anchors at t = 0.** The cover portraits and the Plato bust are
  visible before the first word; no empty ring is left on screen.
- **One image is one block.** Each portrait keeps its ring, name, dates and work
  label; the frame never arrives before the face.
- **Order is the argument.** Slide 10 was re-ordered to **Engels 1848 → Marx
  1867 → Lenin 1917**, so the three timeline dots sit under the work each face
  carries. T066 had 1848 under Marx and 1867 under Engels.
- **No coda image.** Every picture first appears at `t=0` or on the sentence
  that names it. The last click of slide 10 draws the timeline, not a face.
- **Cut-outs stay.** The portraits are still transparent cut-outs; the defect
  was timing and ground, not the cut-out.

## Motion laws applied

- One hero move per slide; at most two movers per click.
- A Morph carries a state change; it is not a transition between unrelated
  slides. Each pair shares 7–16 `!!` names (A 13, B 16, C 15, D 7).
- Titles never animate inside a slide. They are on the base plate; the cross-
  slide change is the Morph.
- The deck has **3 object lifecycles** (slide 11 dims three tiles); it is not a
  deck that only adds.
