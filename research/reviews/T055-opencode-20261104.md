## Findings

### R1 — Blocking — the task does not build outside one machine

- **Evidence:** `inputs/make_product_glb.py:33-37` reads
  `C:\Users\COMPUTER\AppData\Local\Temp\opencode\skill-for-pptx-T054\…\blockout.json`;
  `preview/glb-manifest.json` stores the same absolute path. Any worktree
  without the T054 clone fails at import time.
- **Fix:** freeze `blockout.json` as an input artifact and build from it.
  **Applied by T064:** `inputs/blockout.json` (12,357 B, SHA-256
  `86bf30f8…cd389b`, copied from T054 `b387146`); the rebuilt GLB is
  byte-identical, so no other evidence changed.

### R2 — Major — a negative result was produced by an argument-order bug

- **Evidence:** `inputs/morph_video.py:31` calls
  `pres.CreateVideo(mp4, 3, 3, 30, 85)`. The signature is
  `(FileName, UseTimingsAndNarrations, DefaultSlideDuration, VertResolution,
  FramesPerSecond, Quality)`, so `30` was the resolution and `85` the frame
  rate. The observed 52 × 28 clip is exactly this bug.
- **Effect:** "CreateVideo produced a 52 × 28 clip" is not evidence that
  PowerPoint cannot render the deck.
- **Fix:** correct the call. **Applied by T064** and recorded in the T055
  report's negative results with attribution.

### R3 — Major — the claim overstates what the deck contains

- **Evidence:** the claim file promises "named camera views and Morph"; the
  deck uses one camera `(-0.85, 0.45, 2.10)` on all three slides, and the
  re-audit finds no `<p:transition>` in the package.
- **Fix:** the author restates the claim as "one authored camera, three
  annotated views". Morph moves to its own claim only when it is shown.

### R4 — Major — unverified Morph decks are committed as artifacts

- **Evidence:** `artifacts/morph-3d.pptx` and `artifacts/morph-3d-saved.pptx`
  (911 KB together) record a transition the report says was never observed.
  They are 28 % of the task's artifact weight and contradict the honest
  negative result.
- **Fix:** delete them, or keep one and label it "package structure only". T064
  supplies the frames that settle the question.

### R5 — Major — evidence stores machine-absolute paths

- **Evidence:** `preview/glb-manifest.json` `"source"` and `"glb"` were
  `C:\Users\…\Temp\opencode\skill-for-pptx-T055\…`.
- **Fix:** **Applied by T064** — relative paths plus a provenance block
  (origin, commit, SHA-256). The diff touches only those fields.

### R6 — Minor — camera study and final camera do not match

- **Evidence:** the report says the deck uses study view B, but
  `preview/camera-study.json` B is `(-0.85, 0.45, 2.5)` and the deck uses
  `(-0.85, 0.45, 2.10)`; the manifest and the re-audit agree on 2.10.
- **Fix:** state that the final camera is a closer variant of B, or re-render
  the study at 2.10.

### R7 — Minor — a size threshold is a weak assertion

- **Evidence:** `tests/test_product_deck_3d.py:189-191` accepts the PDF on
  `size > 100_000`. A blank PDF of the right length would pass.
- **Fix:** assert page count and that the model region is non-empty (T064's
  `pdf_page.py` shows the check is cheap).

### R8 — Minor — claim scope names files that do not exist

- **Evidence:** the claim lists `scripts/product_deck_forge.py` and
  `scripts/powerpoint_3d_bridge.ps1`; neither is in the branch. All code lives
  in `experiments/…/inputs/` and `tests/`.
- **Fix:** correct the scope. Keeping the work out of product paths is right
  for an unreviewed capability; the claim should say so.

### R9 — Minor — lighting conclusions are not testable by the suite

- **Evidence:** "a black horizontal face renders grey", "metallic renders as a
  white mirror" are prose observations backed by PNGs; no test would catch a
  regression after a PowerPoint update.
- **Fix:** keep as prose, but date the PowerPoint build in the claim
  (`16.0.17932` here) so a future regression is attributable.

### R10 — Process — same-family review

- **Evidence:** author and reviewer are both DeepSeek V4.1 Flash agents.
- **Fix:** none in code; the owner should count this as a second pass, not as
  the different-family review `lab/standards/REVIEW.md` asks for.

## Validity, overlap, domain fit

- **Validity:** the contract tests are real package tests on the committed
  deck, not synthetic fixtures. They could pass while the renderer is broken —
  which is exactly why R7 matters. The rung-4/5 evidence (COM audit, PDF) is
  independent of the tests and reproduces.
- **Overlap:** none. No canonical module duplicates this; `morph_studio.py`
  owns Morph transitions for 2D shapes but nothing owns 3D models. No product
  path is touched.
- **Domain fit:** the work uses PowerPoint's native `am3d:model3d`, COM camera
  properties, native text and shapes. The imported idea (SolidCraft CSG from
  the vector domain) was tested in the receiving domain: PowerPoint renders it,
  and the PDF proves it. That meets the cross-craft rule in `AGENTS.md`.
- **Regressions:** none observed; the 10 tests and the full suite pass after
  the rebase.
