## Domain fit

- Editing on a copy of the original, reordering through `sldIdLst`, restyling a native table and
  swapping a picture's image part all keep PowerPoint's own objects and ids. That is what keeps
  animation alive.
- The two-tier scoring keeps D001: gates say content survived, eyes say whether it is good, and the
  four parts are not summed.
- Guide 1's "ask until you understand" and "when to ask again" are written from the owner's own words.
  They have not been tried by a small model yet.
